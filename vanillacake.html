Vanilla Cake

DryIngredients:
All-purpose flour or cake flour: 2 ½ cups (approx. 300g–350g) for structure.Baking powder: 2 to 2 ½ teaspoons to help the cake rise.Salt: ½ teaspoon to balance the sweetness.Granulated white sugar: 1 ½ to 2 cups (approx. 300g–400g) for sweetness and moisture.
Wet Ingredients:
Unsalted butter: ½ cup (115g), softened at room temperature for rich flavor.Vegetable oil: ½ cup or a few tablespoons (depending on the recipe) to keep the crumb extra moist.Eggs: 3 to 4 large eggs at room temperature to bind and aerate the batter.Vanilla extract: 1 to 2 tablespoons of high-quality extract for the core flavor.Milk or Buttermilk: 1 cup at room temperature for liquid balance and tender texture.

Allergens

Contains: Eggs, Milk, Wheat (Gluten).
May contain traces of: Tree Nuts, Peanuts, Soy.
